# Cross-axis coherence rubric

## Owner

This final owner judges cross-axis coherence after documentation, observability, architecture, and behavioral-evidence axes have produced normalized results for the same candidate binding.

## Criteria

### COH-1. Preserve every axis disposition

Coherence must not upgrade, erase, or weaken any axis non-pass. A `block`, `indeterminate`, or `unavailable` result remains mechanically non-passing regardless of this review.

### COH-2. Reconcile the same evidence and candidate

Use all axis results, deterministic evidence, and resulting content from the same candidate binding. Do not introduce evidence from another revision or assume later repair.

### COH-3. Identify cross-axis contradictions

Block additional contradictions visible only across owners: documentation promising behavior tests do not prove, behavioral changes bypassing architecture boundaries, observability claims inconsistent with actual failure behavior, or individually acceptable changes that combine into an incoherent contract.

### COH-4. Do not rerun focused ownership

Do not replace or independently rescore focused axes. Add a coherence blocker only when interaction among axis findings or resulting-tree consequences creates a distinct unresolved problem.

## Evidence expectations

Cite contributing axis findings and concrete paths or deterministic evidence. If no additional cross-axis conflict exists, pass without weakening or rewriting axis results.
